About Orlena

Established in 2018, under the umbrella of PT Orlena Delapan Mulia, Orlena is a dessert brownie brand from Bali. Rooted in quality, innovation, and exploration, we strongly committed to our vision to become a leading accessible brownies brand in Indonesia. Our mission is to make indulgence feel effortless through approachable pricing, availability, and formats, so everyone can be happy easily.

Our Story

Orlena was founded in 2018 by Putu Metta Puspita Dewi at the age of 15, starting from a small home kitchen.

She knew nothing about business at the time, but was driven by her passion for desserts and her intention to make people happy through indulgence.

As the products grew more deeply into people’s hearts, Orlena grew from a home kitchen into a proper brand, expanding its presence to six branches, including one in a mall and two café experience stores.

All made possible by Frenna’s love for Orlena.
